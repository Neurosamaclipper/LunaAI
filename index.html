Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how LunaAI ("the Bot", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and stores data when you interact with our Discord application.

1. Data We Collect

To operate effectively, LunaAI collects minimal necessary information from Discord servers and users:

  • User Identifiers: Your Discord User ID, username, and avatar URL to identify you during interactions and maintain user preferences/conversations.
  • Server & Channel Identifiers: Server (Guild) IDs and Channel IDs where LunaAI is added or used, enabling localized server settings and responses.
  • Command & Message Content: Text inputs provided directly in commands, prompts, or messages directed at LunaAI (e.g., via slash commands or direct mentions) to process AI requests.
  • Usage Logs: Command usage statistics, timestamps, and error logs for maintaining performance and troubleshooting.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not continuously read, log, or store ambient chat messages in channels where LunaAI is present unless explicitly triggered by a command or mention.
  • We do not collect personal payment details (all transactions are handled securely by third-party providers like Stripe or Discord App Subscriptions).

3. How We Use Your Data

We use the collected information solely for the following purposes:

  • To process AI requests and provide responses.
  • To store your user preferences (e.g., custom settings or thread memory).
  • To monitor system health, prevent abuse, and optimize bot performance.

4. Third-Party Data Sharing

LunaAI relies on third-party AI APIs (such as OpenAI, Anthropic, or custom endpoints) to process user prompts. Prompts sent to LunaAI are transmitted securely to these service providers strictly for generating responses. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to advertisers or third parties.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

  • Prompt Data: Conversation history and prompt memory are retained only as long as necessary to maintain contextual conversations or server configuration.
  • Deletion Requests: You may request the deletion of your stored data at any time by contacting us through our Support Server or issuing the bot's data reset commands (if enabled). Server owners can remove all server-specific data by kicking the bot from the server.

6. Contact

If you have questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to request data deletion, please contact us via our Discord Support Server or at support@lunaai-example.com.
